Our Origin Story

The Story Behind Taste of Africa

Every great restaurant starts with a recipe, but Taste of Africa started with a family, a passion for cooking, and a belief that food has the power to bring people together.

Many years ago, in a lively Ghanaian neighborhood, there was a woman everyone knew as Maame Afia. Her kitchen was famous. From the rich aroma of jollof rice to the sound of kelewele sizzling in the pan, something delicious was almost always cooking.

Family members would arrive “just to say hello” and somehow leave with a full stomach. Neighbors began stopping by for a plate of food, and friends started bringing their friends. Before long, Maame Afia's little kitchen had become a meeting place for everyone who loved good Ghanaian food.

One evening, after watching yet another group of people gather around her dining table, her son laughed and said, “Maame, you are already feeding half of Ghana. Why don't we open a restaurant?” That simple joke planted an idea.

With a few tables, traditional family recipes, and plenty of determination, Taste of Africa was born.

The early days were not easy. There were quiet afternoons, long nights, and plenty of lessons along the way. But every customer was welcomed like family. Plates were served generously, stories were shared across tables, and the food always carried the warmth of home.

Soon, people began telling others about the little restaurant where the food tasted like home and everyone felt like family. What started with one family's love for cooking grew into a celebration of African food and culture.

Today, Taste of Africa brings together traditional Ghanaian favorites and the rich flavors of Africa, while keeping the same warmth and hospitality that started it all. Because at Taste of Africa, we believe food is more than something you eat.

It is culture. It is family. It is a story—and every plate tells one.
